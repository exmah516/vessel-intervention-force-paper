How can I update all fields in a document?
Explain some fields are updated while other fields are not
Update fields Word issues for me.
Sometimes you need to manually trigger an update of the information in fields like those used in a table of contents, headers and footers, cross-references, bookmarks, and formulas in tables. You can update fields one by one, or you can update all the fields in your document at once.
In this article
Update a field
Update all fields in a document
Update a field
In a document you have open, right-click a field, such as a table of contents, a page number, or a cross-reference, and click
Update Field
.
Note
If you're updating a table of contents, in the
Update Table of Contents
dialog box, select whether you want to update page numbers only or the entire table. Updating page numbers only simply updates the page numbers for the existing sections. It doesn't add new sections to your table of contents. To update your table of contents with new sections from your document, select
Update entire table
.
